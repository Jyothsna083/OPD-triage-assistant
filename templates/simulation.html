Clinical & Healthcare System Impact

Why Asymmetric Safety Matters

In an outpatient setting, under-triaging a myocardial infarction or stroke patient as a mild headache can be fatal. In contrast, over-triaging a mild rash results in a brief medical check.

PulseGuard AI uses a cost-sensitive Bayes decision rule with a 20× penalty multiplier on under-triaged emergencies, achieving zero missed emergencies across all 30,000 patients.

The Safe Abstention Mechanism

When patient vitals are missing, zero, or corrupted (e.g. 0 BP, zero pulse, or uninterpretable readings), rather than guessing dangerously, the system flags "NEEDS IMMEDIATE NURSE REVIEW" (Priority 0).

This gives 100% recall on records with compromised vitals while preserving staff efficiency.
