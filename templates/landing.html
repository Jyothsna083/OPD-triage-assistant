Safety-First Architecture

Built with Zero Compromise on Patient Safety

Designed for real-world government hospital conditions where vitals may be noisy, missing, or multilingual.

⚖️

Asymmetric 20× Penalty

Standard ML treats all errors equally. PulseGuard AI applies a 20× cost penalty for under-triaging emergencies, eliminating fatal false negatives.

🚨

Hard-Coded Red Flag Rules

Life-threatening indicators (SpO2 < 90%, SBP < 80, ACS chest pain triad, FAST stroke) deterministically escalate the patient. The ML model cannot downgrade them.

🗣️

Multilingual Clinical NLP

Parses chief complaints entered in English, Hinglish, and Telugu-English transliteration, featuring clause-level negation and severity detection.

🛑

Safe Abstention Protocol

When ≥ 2 vital signs are corrupted or missing, the system abstains with "Cannot assess — send to nurse now" and assigns Priority 0 for immediate triage.
